# Historical map data

data/history-areas.json holds the area of each civilization in data/history.js, cut from the
Historical Basemaps project (https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps) with scripts/build-history-areas.js,
and rounded to 0.1 degree. That project is licensed GPL-3.0; the maps on the site credit and link to it.
The project says its historical boundaries are approximate and uncertain, and so does every page that uses them.
